## ARCHIVED ALTERNATIVE BLOCK: fully stripped version of Section 10

**Status: archived option. This is NOT the live text, is NOT part of the published Refund Policy, and is NOT to be published.**

The live and operative text is Section 10 above, "Correction of charges that are not payments for services". The founder reviewed and approved the recast on 7 September 2026: the correction of a duplicate charge and of an unauthorised charge is kept, and is expressly characterised as not being a refund. That characterisation is what allows Section 5.1 to state, without any carve-out, that no refund is issued for any Uptal service.

The block below removes even those two corrections. It is retained for the record only. If it were ever adopted, the whole of Section 10 above would be replaced by the text below, and the consequential amendments listed at the end of this block would be made throughout the document.

Note, so the choice is made with open eyes: refusing a correction does not keep the money. The issuer takes it anyway on a duplicate-processing or unauthorised-transaction chargeback, Uptal additionally pays the dispute fee, and the item lands in the Visa and Mastercard monitoring programme numerators. It also runs against the provisions of the Civil Transactions Law under which a person who receives a payment that was not due must return it, which cannot be contracted out of.

### Alternative Section 10: No corrections and no payments of any kind

#### 10.1 The rule

**In plain terms: Uptal makes no payment back to you in any case. There is no refund, and there is no correction either.**

No refund is ever issued for any Uptal product or service, and no payment, repayment, reimbursement, reversal, adjustment, or correction of any Charge is made by Uptal (شركة الخبرات الصاعدة لتقنية المعلومات) in any case, on any ground, and in respect of any billing event, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable Saudi law. This Policy contains no exception, no carve-out, no category, no discretion, and no procedure under which any amount charged is returned to you. Sections 3, 5 and 6 apply to every Charge without exception and without qualification.

#### 10.2 No categories of correction

Without limiting Section 10.1, and to the maximum extent permitted by applicable Saudi law, no payment is made by Uptal (شركة الخبرات الصاعدة لتقنية المعلومات) in respect of, and no procedure exists under this Policy for: a charge alleged to be a duplicate of another charge, whether or not it resulted from an error in Uptal's billing systems; a charge alleged to have been made without the authority of the holder of the payment method, whether or not that allegation is supported by evidence and whether or not it arises from a compromise of a payment method, a device, credentials, or an Uptal account; a charge alleged to have created no Subscription, order, entitlement, or access; a charge alleged to have been made in an incorrect amount, in an incorrect currency, on an incorrect date, or to an incorrect payment method; and any other billing event of any description.

#### 10.3 Payment methods, account security and disputed charges

You represent and warrant that you are authorised to use each payment method you provide to Uptal (شركة الخبرات الصاعدة لتقنية المعلومات) and that each Charge made to it is authorised by its holder. You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials, for the security of your devices and payment methods, and for all activity and all Charges arising through your account or through any payment method associated with it. Every Charge initiated through your account or through a payment method associated with it is treated as authorised by you for the purposes of this Policy. Where you believe that a Charge was made without your authority, your route is to your bank, card issuer, or payment provider under their own rules and under applicable Saudi law, and not to Uptal (شركة الخبرات الصاعدة لتقنية المعلومات) under this Policy.

#### 10.4 Notification

You should notify Uptal (شركة الخبرات الصاعدة لتقنية المعلومات) by a Ticket submitted at https://uptal.com/contact-us, selecting the "Billing & payments" reason, of any Charge you consider to be incorrect, within thirty (30) calendar days of the date of the Charge, stating the transaction reference, the amount, the currency, the date, the payment method used, and a description of the circumstances. Uptal (شركة الخبرات الصاعدة لتقنية المعلومات) will record and investigate the notification, will secure the account where a compromise is reported, and will correct any error in your account records, in your entitlements, or in your future billing. No such notification, investigation, correction, or response creates or evidences any right to any payment, and Uptal (شركة الخبرات الصاعدة لتقنية المعلومات) makes no payment on any such notification. Uptal's determination concludes Uptal's internal process, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable Saudi law and without prejudice to your right to refer the matter to the competent courts in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, or to make a complaint to any competent authority in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. This period is a condition of Uptal's internal review process and does not shorten, and does not purport to shorten, any period prescribed by law for bringing a claim before a competent court or for making any request to a bank, card issuer, payment provider, or card scheme.

#### 10.5 Your rights under mandatory Saudi law

Nothing in this Section limits, excludes, or purports to limit or exclude any right or remedy conferred on you by a provision of the law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia that is mandatory and cannot be waived, limited, or varied by agreement, including any obligation to return an amount received without lawful cause, and this Section applies subject to and to the maximum extent permitted by such law. Sections 1.4 and 15.3 apply to this Section. Where a mandatory provision of Saudi law requires an amount to be returned, that requirement operates only to the minimum extent that the law concerned requires, the amount is calculated in the manner provided by Section 15.3, and it is paid to the Payer of Record, to the original payment method, in the original currency of the Charge, and in no other manner. Any payment so made is made in discharge of that mandatory obligation alone; it is not a refund of any Uptal service, is not an admission of any entitlement, and creates no precedent and no course of dealing.
