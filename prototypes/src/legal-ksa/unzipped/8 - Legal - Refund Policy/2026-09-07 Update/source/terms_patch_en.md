# Terms & Conditions patch (English), companion to the Refund Policy of September 7, 2026

**Status: this patch must ship in the same release as the new Refund Policy.** The Refund Policy prevails over the Terms only on billing matters; on everything else the Terms are the senior instrument and the Policy is incorporated into them by reference. Publishing the Policy without these amendments leaves the deleted guarantee alive by incorporation, leaves a renewal pricing clause that authorises a charge the customer never authorised, and leaves a SAR 100 liability cap against a seat that costs roughly SAR 17,976 a year.

**How to apply.** Nine amendments, applied to the live English Terms & Conditions article, plus one release edit to the closing date line. Each "CURRENT" block below is quoted verbatim from the live text, and each is unique in the document, so it can be matched exactly and replaced with the "REPLACE WITH" block. Do not paraphrase, do not renumber sections, do not merge amendments. Amendments 7 and 9 each contain two separate edits, labelled (a) and (b); apply both.

**Note on the source text.** The live article carries a leading space at the start of each paragraph and a trailing space at the end of each line. Match on the sentence text only, ignoring that surrounding whitespace, and preserve whatever whitespace convention surrounds the sentence you are replacing.

**Note on the retired support email address.** The literal address `support@uptal.com` appears in this file ONLY inside "CURRENT" blocks, because those blocks are the search strings you match against the published page. It appears in no "REPLACE WITH" block and is never published. The Arabic patch follows the same convention. After this patch is applied the retired address must not appear anywhere in the published Terms, and the verification checklist at the end tests for that.

**House rules observed in every replacement below:** no em dashes and no en dashes; all contact is https://uptal.com/contact-us; merchant identity is written exactly as Uptal (شركة الخبرات الصاعدة لتقنية المعلومات); no promise of a job, an interview, or an offer.

**Arabic parity.** Terms Section 23 publishes both language versions and gives the Arabic version precedence. The mirror Arabic patch is a separate deliverable and must publish in the same release, or the prevailing version of the Terms will still contain every defect this patch removes.

## Amendment 1. Terms Section 5 (User accounts)

### CURRENT

> You must notify us promptly at support@uptal.com if you suspect any unauthorized access to or use of your account, and, pending such notification and Uptal's reasonable opportunity to act on it, you shall remain responsible for all activity under your account.

### REPLACE WITH

> You must notify us promptly, by submitting a ticket at https://uptal.com/contact-us, if you suspect any unauthorized access to or use of your account, and, pending such notification and Uptal's reasonable opportunity to act on it, you shall remain responsible for all activity under your account. Notice is given in the manner set out in Section 22.

### WHY

The Terms would otherwise direct a user reporting account compromise to an address that is being retired, so the report goes into an unmonitored mailbox and the user acquires a genuine grievance and a strong dispute narrative on the very facts that produce unauthorized charges.

### REPLACE WITH

The deleted sentence is a standing admission in the senior instrument that Uptal considers refunds, which an arbitrator reads as the operative rule and which contradicts the Policy's central statement that no refund is ever issued for any Uptal service.

## Amendment 4. Terms Section 6.3 (Pricing, VAT & price changes)

### CURRENT

> The price displayed at checkout, or the then-current price for your plan as published on the Website at the time of renewal, is the price in effect for that charge.

### REPLACE WITH

> The price in effect for a new purchase is the price displayed to you at checkout for that purchase. The authority you give for a subscription is an authority to charge a specific stated amount at a specific stated interval, both of which are displayed to you at checkout, accepted by you before payment, recorded in the confirmation of your purchase, and shown in your Uptal account. Each renewal charge is made in that same authorised amount, at that same authorised interval. A change to the price of a plan applies to the renewals of a subscription you already hold only from the point at which the new price has been displayed to you as the amount that will apply to your future renewals and has been accepted by you by a separate affirmative act, which together form a refreshed authorisation, as occurs on an upgrade, a downgrade, a plan change, a change of billing frequency, an addition or removal of a seat, a reactivation, a resumption after a pause, or a re-entry or replacement of your payment details where the new amount is displayed and separately accepted at that time. Until a refreshed authorisation is given, each renewal charge for that subscription is made in the amount last authorised by you for that plan. The automatic updating, re-issue, or replacement of card credentials, whether by you, by your card issuer, or by an account updater service operated by a card scheme or payment provider, is not a refreshed authorisation and does not change the amount you have authorised. Section 13.2 of the Refund Policy governs this Section.

### WHY

This is the critical one: the deleted wording lets a renewal be charged at a published price the subscriber never authorised, which is exactly the two-limb precondition for a payer's demand for a refund within eight weeks of a debit under Article 90 of the SAMA Implementing Regulations, so leaving it restores inside the Terms the single exposure the new Refund Policy is built to close, while the first sentence of Section 6.3 preserves Uptal's freedom to change prices at any time without prior notice.
